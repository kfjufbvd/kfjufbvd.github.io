Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy sets forth our complete data‑handling framework for Count Ball Time, a ball‑timing puzzle mobile application (hereinafter referred to as “the Application”). By downloading, installing, accessing or interacting with this Application, you confirm you have carefully read, fully understood, and voluntarily accept all data‑processing rules laid out in this document. All terms used within this policy share the same definition scope as our Terms of Service unless explicitly defined otherwise. We collect user information strictly following data‑minimization rules, and personal data will only be used for delivering, optimizing and securing our in‑app services. We will never share or sell your personal details to external parties except for scenarios clearly specified in this Privacy Policy.

1. Categories of Information We Gather

We only collect datasets genuinely required to guarantee stable puzzle gameplay, support cross‑device sync, run anti‑abuse monitoring and enhance overall user experience. All collection activities comply with prevailing local data‑protection regulations. The main information categories are shown below:

•                      Account and Profile Credentials: When you log‑in through supported third‑party authorization channels such as Apple ID or Google Sign‑In, we obtain your authorized email address, public display alias and profile avatar image. Such information is exclusively applied to complete identity verification, initialize your in‑app player profile, and synchronize ball‑timing challenge archives, unlocked props and user preferences across multiple mobile devices. We will not fetch extra private content irrelevant to account registration.

•                      Advertising‑Related Device Identifiers: We gather your device‑level advertising identifiers (IDFA for iOS operating systems, GAID for Android operating systems). These identifiers cooperate with our ad‑service partners to execute anti‑fraud validation, adjust ad display relevance, and deliver rewarded in‑app missions. No real‑world personally identifiable information will be bound to these advertising identifiers.

•                      Network and Hardware‑Related Metadata:

￮     Public IP address: Used to infer broad network‑access regions without pinpointing your precise physical location, meanwhile helping us block suspicious login behaviors and malicious network intrusions.

￮     Core device parameters: including hardware model, operating system release version, screen display settings, system‑preferred language, time‑zone configuration, application launch timestamps and active session duration. This metadata helps us adapt ball‑timing puzzle layouts for diversified hardware and resolve cross‑platform compatibility failures.

•                      In‑App Behavioral Records and Diagnostic Logs: The Application automatically generates and stores usage logs during your gameplay. This covers ball‑timing challenge statistics, attempt counts, completion timings, hint‑tool utilization frequency, level‑unlocking progress, crash snapshots and runtime error codes. We leverage these records to observe service operating status, reproduce technical defects rapidly, calibrate puzzle difficulty curves and continuously polish player experience.

2. How We Process and Utilize Collected Information

Every piece of gathered data shall only be processed for the lawful service‑oriented purposes listed below. We will not repurpose your information for unstated commercial scenarios unless you provide separate explicit authorization:

•                      Deliver and sustain core Application capabilities: Rely on your account credentials to validate identity and sync ball‑timing challenge records, finished puzzle stages, virtual props and reward inventory among your logged‑in devices. If reward‑redemption modules are available within the Application, relevant account markers will be used to guarantee accurate reward distribution. We also leverage advertising identifiers according to ad‑provider requirements to render advertisements and watch‑to‑earn task entries.

•                      Deliver service‑related notifications: We may send emails bound to your registered account under specific conditions, such as acknowledging your account‑deletion submissions, notifying you of material revisions to this Privacy Policy or Terms of Service, and reminding you of major in‑app event launches.

•                      Carry out customer‑support workflows: When you submit service tickets for issues including progress loss, abnormal reward redemption or Application crashes, your registered email serves as our communication channel. We can analyze device‑related details you submit to reproduce glitches and accelerate troubleshooting efficiency.

3. Permitted Scenarios for Information Disclosure

We attach high priority to personal‑data security. Your personal identifiable information will not be disclosed to external entities except for the following compliance‑aligned and necessary circumstances:

•                      Sharing with authorized ad‑service vendors: Your advertising identifiers may be transferred to trusted ad‑cooperation partners for advertisement rendering, exposure‑effect evaluation and rewarded‑task implementation. These vendors are restrained by formal contractual confidentiality clauses and are forbidden to reuse these identifiers for business outside the scope of serving Count Ball Time.

•                      Corporate‑transaction data transfer: In cases of business merger, asset acquisition, enterprise sale or bankruptcy liquidation, user datasets may be passed to successor entities as business assets. We will enforce successor parties to abide by this Privacy Policy’s protection standards, and issue in‑app or email notifications for such transfers when local laws require.

•                      Disclosure compelled by legal requirements: We may output limited‑scope user information responding to valid judicial subpoenas, administrative orders or official regulatory demands. Under such circumstances, only the minimum dataset satisfying legal obligations will be provided, and notification will be delivered unless forbidden by law provisions.

4. Technical and Administrative Data‑Security Safeguards

Multiple‑layer security mechanisms are deployed to defend your personal information against unauthorized reading, tampering, leakage and improper destruction:

•                      Transmission and storage encryption: All data transmitted between your mobile terminal and our backend servers adopts TLS 1.3 encryption protocols to resist interception and tampering during transmission. User personal archives stored on our servers are protected with AES‑256 static encryption.

•                      Strict access‑permission management: Access to non‑public personal information is strictly limited to internal staff and contracted third‑party collaborators with legitimate business needs. All relevant personnel must sign binding non‑disclosure agreements and will bear accountability for information‑leak incidents.

•                      Periodic security inspection mechanism: We conduct recurring vulnerability scanning, security audit and penetration testing for servers, databases and Application clients to detect and fix latent risks. Application and server software will be updated periodically to counter emerging cybersecurity threats. Please note that no internet‑based transmission or electronic‑storage solution can achieve absolute security. We adopt all commercially reasonable protective measures but cannot guarantee one‑hundred‑percent risk‑free data safety.

5. Data Retention Specifications

We preserve personal information only within the time span necessary for fulfilling collection objectives or satisfying statutory retention duties. Concrete retention rules are as follows:

•                      General retention principle: When your personal data is no longer required for service delivery or legal compliance (for example, your account remains continuously inactive for 12 months), we will execute data deletion or anonymization procedures.

•                      Retention after account‑deletion approval: Once your account‑deletion application passes verification, most of your identifiable information including email, display name and profile avatar will be erased right away. For anti‑fraud considerations such as preventing repeated reward‑claim abuse described in our Terms of Service, partial non‑identifiable device‑related markers may be reserved for a maximum of six months and will be cleared once anti‑risk monitoring finishes.

•                      Anonymized statistical‑data preservation: After erasing personal identifiers, aggregated anonymous datasets (for instance, puzzle‑challenge completion‑rate statistics, device‑model distribution) may be retained long‑term for product‑optimization work; such datasets cannot trace back to any individual end‑user.

6. Procedures for Account and Associated‑Data Deletion

You possess the permanent right to apply for full erasure of your account and linked personal data through two alternative submission channels:

  1.                 In‑App Self‑Service Request: Launch Count Ball Time, navigate to Settings > Account & Privacy, tap “Request Account Deletion”, and complete identity validation following on‑screen guidance. Identity verification via your registered email may be required to secure your request.
  1.                 Email‑Submission Request: Send your deletion‑request email to aoalobtu@outlook.com, subject line: Count Ball Time Account and Full‑Data Deletion Request. Please provide your registered email or account ID for identity confirmation. Our team will finish reviewing your application within 7 business days and feed back processing outcomes via email. Once approved, data removal will be implemented in accordance with the above‑mentioned retention specifications.

Please keep in mind: after account‑data deletion completes, all ball‑timing challenge progress, virtual props, reward records and account‑profile content will be permanently lost and cannot be recovered.

7. Runtime Diagnostic Log Data

When runtime exceptions such as crashes or freezing occur within Count Ball Time, integrated third‑party diagnostic utilities will collect anonymous log information automatically. These log datasets may contain your public IP address, device hardware name and model, operating‑system version, Application runtime parameters (including current puzzle stage, client version number, ad‑SDK operating state), error‑occurrence timestamps, error codes and fault‑reproduction‑related statistical metrics. Log collection serves exclusively for fault diagnosis, bug elimination and Application‑stability improvement. These logs do not carry personally identifiable markers and will not be diverted to other usage scenarios.

You may adjust acceptance‑refusal settings for Cookies inside your device‑system configurations. Disabling Cookies may cause partial ad‑relevant functions to operate abnormally.

•                      Contact‑list permission (explicit user consent mandatory): To carry out anti‑fraud detection and optional friend‑invite functions, the Application may pop‑up a permission request for accessing your device contact list. Contact‑list content will only be uploaded to servers after you explicitly grant authorization. Contact information will be deleted immediately once corresponding service purposes are accomplished and will never be shared with third‑party institutions without your separate consent.
