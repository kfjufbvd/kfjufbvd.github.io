Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy sets forth our full data-processing framework for Walk Pace Metronome, a mobile fitness application built for walking cadence training (hereinafter referred to as "the Application"). By downloading, installing, launching or interacting with this Application, you affirm you have carefully reviewed, fully understood, and voluntarily accept every provision laid out within this document. We maintain the authority to revise this Privacy Policy to adapt to feature upgrades, platform compliance specifications and updated local regulatory requirements. All users are encouraged to visit the privacy page inside Application Settings regularly to stay informed of policy adjustments.

1. Information We Collect

We abide by strict data-minimization standards. Only datasets essential to sustain metronome-driven cadence training, preserve user preset configurations, stabilize application performance and execute risk-control mechanisms will be gathered. No unnecessary private content will be obtained without your explicit permission. Specific data categories are listed below:

•                      Account & User Profile Details: When you opt for third-party sign-in services including Apple ID or Google Sign-In, we receive your authorized email address, public display alias and profile picture. This information is exclusively used to initialize your user profile, complete identity validation, and sync metronome presets, walking-training records and custom rhythm configurations across multiple devices. We will never access extra private materials irrelevant to account creation.

•                      Device and Network Technical Metadata: The Application automatically collects advertising identifiers (IDFA for iOS, GAID for Android), public IP address, device hardware model, operating system build version, screen resolution, system-preferred language, time-zone setting and active network connection mode (Wi-Fi or cellular). These metadata help us adjust audio-output parameters for different hardware setups, resolve intermittent network anomalies and identify suspicious access behaviors.

•                      Cadence-Training Usage Records: Anonymous usage metrics will be logged locally and on-server, covering metronome tempo preset parameters, walking-session duration, custom rhythm-pattern modifications, training-plan switching frequency, favorite preset collections, as well as interaction events triggered by in-app advertisements and reward tasks. Aggregated anonymous analysis of these records helps us optimize audio-rhythm logic, enrich preset rhythm libraries and polish overall fitness-training experience.

•                      Performance and Fault Diagnostic Logs: If the Application encounters audio-output interruption, app freezing, loading failure or runtime exceptions, the system will automatically generate diagnostic logs, error codes and device runtime snapshots. Such logs serve only to pinpoint technical defects and roll out subsequent version fixes, and contain no personally-identifiable user markers.

•                      Optional Installed-Application Catalogue Permission (User Consent Mandatory): Upon the very first launch, integrated advertising SDKs may pop-up a permission request to read your locally-installed app catalogue. You possess full authority to reject this request. Denying permission will not impair core metronome cadence-training capabilities. Our development team cannot view or store this catalogue information; all relevant processing operations are independently managed by the corresponding ad service provider subject to its own privacy terms.

2. Lawful Grounds for Data Utilization

Every piece of collected data shall only be processed for service-related legitimate purposes described below. We will not repurpose your information for unrelated commercial scenarios unless you provide separate explicit authorization:

•                      Keep core fitness-training functions running normally: synchronize metronome tempo presets, custom rhythm patterns, historical walking-training logs and user-collected rhythm templates among your logged-in devices.

•                      Optimize audio playback stability, haptic-feedback response and cross-device hardware compatibility for various smartphones and tablets.

•                      Deliver appropriate non-intrusive in-app advertisements and reward-earning tasks with support from device-level advertising identifiers.

•                      Receive, categorize and respond to your feature feedback, rhythm-preset suggestions and technical fault reports submitted through built-in support channels.

•                      Send in-app push notifications for newly-released rhythm templates, limited-time training-plan events, service maintenance reminders and privacy-policy revision announcements.

•                      Detect fake accounts, abnormal repeated reward claiming and automated malicious operations, so as to maintain a fair service environment for all fitness-training users.

3. Permitted Information-Disclosure Scenarios

We firmly commit to never selling, leasing or trading your personally-identifiable information for third-party commercial promotion. Data disclosure may only take place under the following limited and compliance-aligned conditions:

•                      Your device advertising identifier may be shared with officially-cooperated ad partners for advertisement rendering, exposure-effect statistics and rewarded-task matching. All cooperating vendors are bound by formal confidentiality agreements and are prohibited from re-using this identifier for other unrelated business projects.

•                      Aggregated, fully anonymized statistical datasets without any user-traceable tags can be shared with third-party analytical institutions for long-term product iteration and user-experience enhancement.

•                      Trusted authorized service suppliers including cloud-storage operators and crash-diagnosis tool providers may obtain limited operational data required to complete assigned duties, and they must comply with identical privacy-protection standards specified within this document.

•                      We may disclose information of the minimum necessary scope when receiving formal court subpoenas, regulatory-investigation orders or mandatory legal demands issued by competent official authorities.

4. Multi-Layer Data-Security Safeguards

We deploy combined technical and administrative protective measures to defend your stored and transmitted information against unauthorized access, tampering, leakage and permanent loss:

•                      All data transmitted between your mobile terminal and our backend servers is encrypted by TLS protocol; account-related archives kept on-server are protected with high-strength static encryption technology.

•                      Tiered internal access-permission controls are implemented: only maintenance and support staff with legitimate job-related requirements can view partial user data, and all relevant personnel must sign binding non-disclosure commitments.

•                      We carry out periodic vulnerability scanning, server-security audits and Application security-patch updates to cope with continuously emerging cybersecurity threats.

Please note that no electronic-storage or internet-transmission technology can achieve absolute data security. We adopt all commercially-feasible protective countermeasures to safeguard your privacy; nonetheless, complete data safety cannot be fully guaranteed under all complex network surroundings.

5. Data Retention Specifications

We preserve your information only for the shortest valid period required to fulfil collection objectives or satisfy legal-archiving obligations:

•                      Your login-account details, cadence-training logs and custom metronome-preset archives will be retained while your account stays active. Once your deletion application passes verification, all these identifiable datasets will be erased from servers instantly.

•                      Anonymous training-session logs and fault-diagnostic records will be stored for a maximum of 9 months; afterwards, such data will be automatically anonymized or thoroughly cleared.

•                      Partial non-identifiable anti-fraud markers may be reserved for a short-term monitoring cycle to prevent reward-claim abuse and will be permanently deleted once risk-monitoring procedures finish.

•                      Data that must be preserved for fulfilling local regulatory compliance requirements will be kept until the statutory retention cycle expires.

6. Account and Full-Data Deletion Procedures

You hold the permanent right to apply for complete erasure of your account and all attached user-related data at any time through two independent submission channels:

  1.                 In-App Self-Service Operation: Launch Walk Pace Metronome, navigate to Settings > Account & Privacy, select the Delete My Account option and strictly follow on-screen prompts to submit your identity-verified deletion request.
  1.                 Email-Based Application: Send an application email to our dedicated support mailbox with the precise subject line: Walk Pace Metronome Full Account Deletion Request. Please attach your registered login email address for identity-verification purposes.

Our support team will complete identity validation and execute full-data-removal workflows within 14 business days, and send a deletion-confirmation email to your registered mailbox. All erased cadence-training logs, custom rhythm presets, reward records and account-profile content cannot be restored under any circumstance.
