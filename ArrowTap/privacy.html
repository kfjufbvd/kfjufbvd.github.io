This Privacy Policy outlines all data collection, processing, storage and disclosure practices applied within ArrowTap - Target Shift mobile game application (hereinafter referred to as “the App”). By downloading, installing, launching or continuously using this App, you fully acknowledge reading, understanding and unconditionally consent to all rules stated in this document. We retain full authority to revise this policy at any time to align with updated service functions, platform specifications or local data protection laws. We advise users to check this policy regularly inside App Settings for any updates.

1. Categories of Information We Collect

We only gather data strictly necessary to maintain stable operation, optimize target-shifting puzzle gameplay and deliver a smooth user experience. No redundant private data will be collected without your explicit authorization. All collected data falls into five separate categories below:

  1.                 Login & Account Profile Information
    If you choose to log in through authorized third-party platforms including Apple Sign-In, Google Account or Facebook Login, we will receive your bound email address, public display name and profile avatar image. This set of data is solely used to create your exclusive game account, complete identity verification and synchronize target-shift puzzle archives across multiple mobile devices. We will never extract extra personal details irrelevant to account creation.
  1.                 Hardware & Network Technical Data
    The App automatically collects non-sensitive device identifiers including IDFA (iOS) / GAID (Android), public IP address, mobile device model, OS version, screen resolution, default system language, time zone and current network type (Wi-Fi / cellular data). This information helps us adapt arrow target layouts for various screen sizes and resolve abnormal network disconnections during gameplay.
  1.                 Target Shift Game Behavioral Records
    We record anonymous in-game interaction data such as target tap frequency, puzzle clearance speed, level retry counts, hint tool usage, theme skin selection, daily play duration and reward-ad interaction logs. All these records are analyzed anonymously to adjust puzzle difficulty gradients, expand target level packs and refine game operating logic.
  1.                 Crash & Performance Diagnostic Logs
    When the App crashes, freezes, lags or fails to load target boards normally, the system will auto-generate diagnostic logs containing error codes, running memory status and the exact puzzle scene at fault. These logs are only utilized to locate technical defects and push subsequent version fixes. No individual user identity markers are attached to these logs.
  1.                 Optional Installed App List Data (Consent Required)
    Our built-in advertising SDK may request permission to view other applications installed on your device upon first launch. You hold absolute right to reject this permission pop-up, and denying access will not block any core target-shift puzzle functions. The development team of ArrowTap - Target Shift cannot access or store this app list data; all relevant processing is independently managed by the ad SDK provider under its own privacy rules.

2. Legitimate Purposes for Data Utilization

Every piece of collected information shall only be processed for service-related legal purposes listed below, and we will never repurpose your data for irrelevant commercial activities without your separate written consent:

•                      Sustain complete core gameplay: sync target shift progress, saved levels, unlocked arrow skins and accumulated in-game rewards across your logged-in devices;

•                      Optimize frame smoothness, tap response sensitivity and layout compatibility for all mainstream mobile hardware;

•                      Match appropriate non-intrusive in-game ads and reward-watch missions with the help of advertising identifiers;

•                      Receive, sort and reply to your feedback, puzzle difficulty suggestions and technical fault reports submitted via the App support channel;

•                      Push in-app notifications about new target level updates, limited arrow skin events and official policy revisions;

•                      Identify fake accounts, repeated reward claim fraud and abnormal tap script abuse to safeguard fair game environment for all players.

3. Circumstances Allowed for Information Sharing

We firmly promise never to sell, rent or trade your personal identifiable data to outside merchants for mass marketing promotion. Data disclosure is only permitted under the following compliant, limited scenarios:

•                      We may share your device advertising identifier with certified ad partners to realize ad rendering, exposure statistics and reward task matching. All cooperative ad providers sign binding confidentiality contracts and are forbidden to reuse this identifier for unrelated business;

•                      Aggregated, fully anonymized statistical data (without any user traceable markers) can be shared with third-party data analysis teams for long-term game iteration and experience upgrade;

•                      Authorized service vendors including cloud storage operators and crash analysis tools can access limited operational data required to fulfill their assigned work, and they must abide by identical privacy protection standards as stated in this policy;

•                      We may disclose minimal necessary information when receiving formal court subpoenas, regulatory investigation orders or mandatory legal demands from competent authorities.

4. Data Security Protection Mechanisms

We deploy multi-layer technical and administrative safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, data tampering, leakage or permanent loss of your information:

•                      All data transmitted between your mobile terminal and our backend server is encrypted via TLS 1.3 transmission protocol; all stored account archives are protected with AES-256 static encryption;

•                      Tiered internal access authority control is implemented: only maintenance and support staff with legitimate job demands can view partial user data, and all relevant personnel sign non-disclosure agreements;

•                      Regular vulnerability scanning, server security audits and App version security patches are scheduled to counter emerging network risks.

Please be reminded that no electronic storage or online transmission technology can guarantee 100% absolute data security. We will take all commercially feasible countermeasures to protect your privacy, but full security cannot be completely guaranteed.

5. Standard Data Retention Rules

We only preserve your information within the shortest time frame required to achieve collection purposes or meet legal retention obligations:

•                      Your login account details, saved target shift levels and skin inventory will be retained during your account active period; once your deletion application is approved, all these identifiable data will be erased instantly;

•                      Anonymous game behavior logs and crash diagnostic records will be stored for a maximum of 9 months, after which they will be automatically anonymized or fully cleared from servers;

•                      Partial non-identifiable anti-fraud markers may be reserved for a limited period to avoid repeated reward abuse, and will be deleted once anti-fraud monitoring is completed;

•                      Data that needs to be archived to comply with tax, accounting or local regulatory rules will be retained until the legal retention cycle expires.
